## What I care about most
- AI and technology, especially how they're changing the labor market and jobs
- Reskilling: how workers, schools, and companies are adapting to AI
- Big tech news: new AI tools, tech companies, tech policy and regulation

## Also interesting (lower priority)
- Climate and clean energy, more solutions than doom
- Space and big science discoveries

## What I'm less interested in
- Celebrity news and gossip
- Sports results
- Political horse-race coverage (polls, campaign drama)

## How I like my news
- I like BOTH opinion pieces and straight news, so give me a mix of the two
- Opinion pieces that argue a clear point, even ones I disagree with
- Explainers and analysis over very short breaking-news updates
- A mix of sources and viewpoints
